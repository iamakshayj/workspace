# Conference, meetup, accelerator, and award package

## Talk title

From Screenshots to Signed Evidence: Building Reproducible Compliance Packs

## Abstract

Compliance reviews often depend on copied screenshots whose source, transformation history, and governing control version are difficult to reconstruct. This talk presents an open reference architecture that normalizes cloud events, correlates related activity with explainable rules, preserves bidirectional lineage, freezes time-valid control snapshots, and signs a canonical manifest for offline verification. A synthetic healthcare scenario demonstrates Azure identity, key management, Kubernetes, CI/CD, and SQL evidence without patient data. The session includes tamper demonstrations, benchmark methodology, threat-model limitations, and practical guidance for adding independent timestamping and hardware-backed keys.

## Learning outcomes

- distinguish integrity, provenance, signer identity, and factual truth;
- design a versioned evidence/control relationship;
- measure correlation precision and false positives rather than claim generic AI accuracy;
- reproduce and verify a signed evidence pack offline.

## Speaker bio

Akshay Jagadeesh is the creator of Verifiable Evidence Core and the Workspace platform. His work focuses on auditable enterprise workflows, cross-system evidence lineage, security-event correlation, and reproducible compliance automation.
